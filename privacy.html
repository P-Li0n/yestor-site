Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Yestor collects, how it is used, and what choices players have.

1. Overview

Yestor is a daily guessing game. It stores gameplay progress and preferences on your device, loads daily moment content from a live public feed, and can sync leaderboard data to a hosted backend so players can appear on shared rankings.

2. Information stored on your device

Yestor stores certain information locally on your device to make the app work properly and preserve your progress. This may include:

  • your completed moments and scores
  • your streaks, unlockables, and app icon selections
  • your appearance settings
  • a locally generated player identifier
  • your leaderboard display name, if you choose or keep one
  • a cached copy of the live daily moment feed

3. Information sent off your device

Yestor may connect to third-party services to load content and support leaderboard features.

  • Daily content feed: Yestor fetches daily moment data from a publicly published Google Sheets CSV feed.
  • Shared leaderboards: if shared leaderboards are enabled in the app build and you complete moments, Yestor may send your leaderboard profile to Supabase. This profile can include your player identifier, display name, completed moments, points, streaks, and leaderboard-related totals.
  • Images and source links: moment photos and related links may load from external URLs included in the editorial feed.

4. Public leaderboard information

If your results are synced to the shared leaderboard, your chosen name, or the auto-generated fallback name assigned by the app, may be visible to other players alongside your scores and ranks. Yestor does not require account registration to create that leaderboard profile.

5. Feedback and request emails

If you choose to send feedback or request a moment by email, your email client will send the information you include to yestor.app@outlook.com. That information is handled by your email provider and the mailbox provider used to receive those messages.

6. What Yestor does not do

  • Yestor does not currently require a user account or password.
  • Yestor does not currently sell personal information.
  • Yestor does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Yestor does not currently use analytics or tracking SDKs for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

7. Data retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device unless it is deleted, reset, or removed when the app is uninstalled. Leaderboard records stored in the backend may remain there until they are updated or removed.

8. Your choices

  • You may keep the app’s auto-generated leaderboard name instead of choosing a custom one.
  • You may update your leaderboard display name from within the app settings.
  • You may choose not to send feedback or request emails.

9. Contact

If you have privacy questions or want to request help related to your data, contact yestor.app@outlook.com.
