Yestor is a daily guessing game built around history, politics, birthdays, music, sports, science, culture, and film. Every day, players study a teaser, image, and escalating clues, then try to place the moment in time.

How it works

Each day starts with a teaser and a blurred image. Wrong guesses reveal stronger clues and sharpen the photo. The closer you get to the exact year, the better your score.

Why Yestor

Yestor is made to feel editorial, tactile, and replay-worthy, with collectible app icons, streaks, unlockables, and shared leaderboards.

Built for daily curiosity

Fast to play, hard to forget, and designed for people who love connecting a clue to a year.
